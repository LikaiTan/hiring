The laboratory of Dr Likai Tan, Department of Anaesthesia and Intensive Care, Faculty of Medicine, The Chinese University of Hong Kong (CUHK), invites applications for PhD and postdoctoral positions. We are seeking candidates with a strong background in immunology and solid practical laboratory skills. Successful applicants will work on research projects supported by the General Research Fund (GRF) of the Hong Kong Research Grants Council (RGC).

01 The laboratory

About the PI and the Laboratory

Dr Likai Tan is a Research Assistant Professor at CUHK. He obtained his PhD in Immunology from Hannover Medical School, Germany, in 2020. He subsequently conducted postdoctoral research at Hannover Medical School and the University Medical Center Hamburg-Eppendorf before joining CUHK in 2023. His research focuses on γδ T-cell biology and systems immunology, with publications in journals including Science Immunology and Cell Reports. His work addresses γδ T-cell development, tissue adaptation and single-cell analysis methods.

The laboratory combines single-cell multi-omics and high-dimensional flow cytometry with functional immune cell assays and organoid co-culture to investigate interactions between γδ T cells and the tissue microenvironment, with a particular focus on γδ T-cell tissue residency, immune regulation and tissue repair.

02 Who can apply

Requirements

  • A strong background in immunology and a keen interest in mechanistic research.
  • Proficiency in English, including scientific reading, writing and academic communication.
  • Practical laboratory experience in immune cell isolation and culture, flow cytometry, functional assays or relevant molecular biology techniques.
  • Willingness to handle potentially infectious clinical samples.
  • Experience in organoid culture, disease modelling or immune cell–organoid co-culture is a strong advantage.

PhD Applicants

Applicants must meet the academic, English-language and other admission requirements of the CUHK Graduate School and the relevant PhD programme, and demonstrate strong research potential. Please refer to the CUHK Graduate School admissions requirements for details.

Outstanding bachelor’s degree holders with research experience may apply for an MPhil as a pathway to PhD study.

Postdoctoral Applicants

Applicants should hold, or be close to completing, a PhD in immunology or a related discipline. They must have published first-author research articles and demonstrate the ability to independently design experiments, advance research projects and prepare manuscripts.

03 Research support

Studentships and Salary

  • PhD students: Full postgraduate studentships are available, with a monthly stipend of approximately HK$19,000. Candidates with outstanding academic achievements and research potential are encouraged to apply for the Hong Kong PhD Fellowship Scheme (HKPFS).
  • Postdoctoral researchers: A monthly salary of approximately HK$37,000 in the first year.

04 Join the laboratory

How to Apply

Please email your CV and a brief statement of your research experience and interests to likaitan@cuhk.edu.hk. Highlight your practical laboratory skills and any experience with organoids. Postdoctoral applicants should include representative first-author publications. PhD applicants may also include academic transcripts and English-language test results.

Please use the subject line “PhD Application – Your Name” or “Postdoctoral Application – Your Name”.

We welcome applicants who are enthusiastic about mechanistic immunology research and interested in combining experimental approaches with single-cell technologies!
